संक्षिप्त हिंदी परिचय (EDITORIAL MISSION)

1. Who We Are & E-E-A-T Editorial Standards

Hindi Anime Guide is an independent Indian digital publication and discovery platform dedicated to Hindi-dubbed anime, simulcast IST broadcast schedules, official manga metadata, and verified industry news. Every title listing, airing schedule, and streaming availability badge is curated to bridge Japanese animation with Hindi-speaking audiences transparently.

2. Zero-Piracy Verification Policy

We maintain a strict, uncompromising anti-piracy standard. Hindi Anime Guide never hosts, embeds, mirrors, or links to unlicensed anime streaming websites, torrent trackers, or unauthorized scanlation portals. Every watch or read button connects exclusively to rights-holding licensors such as Crunchyroll, Netflix, SonyLIV, JioHotstar, Prime Video, Muse India (YouTube), Shueisha MangaPlus, and Viz Media.

3. Automated + Human Data Methodology

Our live IST broadcast countdowns, manga dossier volumes/chapters, and rolling 48-hour newswire sync directly from structured official metadata sources including AniList GraphQL APIs and licensed publisher RSS feeds (Anime News Network, MyAnimeList, Crunchyroll News), reviewed against Indian streaming availability matrices for accuracy.

4. Editorial Contact

For editorial corrections, press releases, licensing partnerships, or advertising inquiries, write directly to contact@hindianimeguide.in or submit a verified inquiry via our Contact & Sponsorships page.
